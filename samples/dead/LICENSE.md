# Dead-note samples

Recorded muted/dead bass noises from Karoryfer Samples, released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain):

- `thumb*.mp3` – "noise_mutedslap" from **Swagbass** (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swagbass)
- `pop*.mp3` – "noise_mutedg" from **Swagbass**
- `soft*.mp3` – "noise_muteda" from **Swagbass**
- `lh*.mp3` – "noise_mutedpalm" from **Fashionbass** (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.fashionbass)

Trimmed to 250 ms and converted to MP3. Thanks to Karoryfer Samples (https://karoryfer.com).
