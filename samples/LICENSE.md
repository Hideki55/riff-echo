# Instrument samples

Single-note renders taken from [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), unmodified:

- Folders without a prefix (e.g. `electric_bass_finger/`, `choir_aahs/`): **Musyng Kite** soundfont,
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `fatboy_*` folders: **FatBoy** soundfont (https://fatboy.site),
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `fluid_*` folders: **FluidR3 GM** soundfont,
  [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).

Files are shared under the same licenses. Drum samples are listed in `drums/LICENSE.md`.
