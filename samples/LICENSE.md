# Instrument samples

The audio files in this folder are single-note renders of the **Musyng Kite** soundfont,
taken from [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

These files are unmodified copies and are shared under the same license.
